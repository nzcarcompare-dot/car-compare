Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025 · Applies to nzcarcompare.co.nz

NZ Car Compare is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect minimal personal data, do not sell your information, and do not require you to create an account or log in.

Who we are

NZ Car Compare (nzcarcompare.co.nz) is a free vehicle cost comparison tool for New Zealand consumers. The site is operated as a sole trader business based in New Zealand. You can contact us at privacy@nzcarcompare.co.nz.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information. No account or login is required.

Vehicle data from Carjam

When you enter a NZ registration plate number, we query the Carjam API to retrieve publicly available vehicle information held in the NZ Transport Agency (NZTA) Motor Vehicle Register. This includes make, model, year, fuel type, safety ratings, fuel economy figures, WOF status, and odometer readings.

This data is sourced from the NZTA register and EECA (Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority) fuel economy testing. Carjam is a licensed data reseller authorised to distribute this information. We display it as provided — we do not modify, enhance, or combine it with other personal data.

Plate numbers you enter are sent to Carjam's servers to retrieve this data. Carjam's own privacy policy applies to their handling of that request: carjam.co.nz/privacy.

Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies to track usage. These are analytics cookies only — we do not use advertising cookies, remarketing cookies, or any tracking that follows you across other websites.

The cookies Google Analytics sets include:

  • _ga — distinguishes unique users, expires after 2 years
  • _ga_XXXXXXXX — maintains session state, expires after 2 years

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Refusing cookies will not affect your ability to use NZ Car Compare.

Third-party services we use

  • Carjam — vehicle data lookups via their ABCD API. Privacy policy
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymised usage analytics. Privacy policy
  • Railway — hosting infrastructure. Your connection data passes through their servers. Privacy policy
  • Cloudflare — DNS and network routing. Privacy policy
  • Trade Me Motors, insurance providers — we link to their sites but do not share any user data with them. Your use of those sites is governed by their own policies.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not store plate numbers beyond the 1-hour cache window
  • We do not create user profiles or track individuals over time
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers
  • We do not use your data for any purpose other than operating and improving this site

Your rights under the NZ Privacy Act 2020

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you, and to request correction of any inaccuracies. Given that we collect no personally identifying information beyond anonymised analytics, most users will have no persistent personal data held by us. To make a request, contact privacy@nzcarcompare.co.nz.

Children's privacy

NZ Car Compare is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
