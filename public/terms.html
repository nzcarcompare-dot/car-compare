What NZ Car Compare does

NZ Car Compare is a free tool that helps New Zealand consumers estimate and compare the total cost of owning two vehicles. Key features include:

  • Comparing up to 5 NZ vehicles by total running costs (fuel, electricity, Road User Charges) and purchase price to calculate break-even points
  • Looking up vehicle details by NZ registration plate using the Carjam API, which sources data from the NZTA Motor Vehicle Register and EECA fuel economy testing
  • Displaying fuel economy figures, CO₂ emissions, safety ratings, odometer readings, and ownership history as provided by Carjam
  • Generating indicative market price estimates based on Trade Me Motors historical averages, adjusted for actual odometer reading using a depreciation model
  • Browsing a database of 43,000+ NZ vehicle variants from the NZ registered fleet with fuel economy and pricing data
  • Linking to third-party services including Trade Me Motors, Carjam (affiliate), and NZ insurance providers

Accuracy of information — please read carefully

NZ Car Compare makes no warranty regarding the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or fitness for purpose of any information on the Site. In particular:

  • Running cost estimates are calculations based on user-provided inputs and displayed fuel/electricity prices. Actual costs vary based on driving conditions, style, maintenance, and fuel price fluctuations.
  • Fuel economy figures are laboratory-tested values (WLTP or equivalent) sourced from EECA via Carjam. Real-world consumption typically differs — often significantly for EVs, hybrids, and PHEVs depending on usage patterns.
  • CO₂ figures represent tailpipe emissions only from manufacturer testing. They do not account for upstream emissions from fuel production or electricity generation.
  • Safety ratings reference ANCAP and other test bodies via Carjam. Ratings apply to the specific tested variant and year — individual vehicles may differ due to modifications, damage, or equipment differences.
  • Vehicle price estimates are calculated from historical Trade Me Motors average sale prices, adjusted using an odometer-based depreciation model (approximately $0.065 per km deviation from expected mileage for the vehicle's age, capped at ±25% of base price). These are indicative estimates only — not valuations, not appraisals, and not offers to buy or sell.
  • Odometer readings are sourced from the NZTA register via Carjam and reflect the most recently reported reading, which may not be current. The actual odometer on the physical vehicle should always be verified.
  • WOF status reflects the most recent result held in the NZTA register and may not be current.
  • Fuel and electricity prices are manually maintained averages and may not reflect current prices in your region.
  • Road User Charges (RUC) are based on current NZTA published rates and are subject to change.
