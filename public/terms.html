What NZ Car Compare does

NZ Car Compare is a free tool that helps New Zealand consumers estimate and compare the total cost of owning two vehicles. Key features include:

  • Comparing estimated running costs (fuel, electricity, Road User Charges) against purchase price to calculate a break-even point
  • Looking up vehicle details by NZ registration plate using the Carjam API, which sources data from the NZTA Motor Vehicle Register and EECA fuel economy testing
  • Displaying fuel economy figures, CO₂ emissions data, and safety ratings as provided by Carjam and the EECA
  • Estimating indicative market prices based on Trade Me Motors historical averages
  • Linking to third-party services including Trade Me Motors and NZ insurance providers

Accuracy of information — please read carefully

NZ Car Compare makes no warranty regarding the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or fitness for purpose of any information on the Site. In particular:

  • Running cost estimates are mathematical calculations based on user-provided inputs and the fuel/electricity prices shown. Actual costs will vary based on your driving style, conditions, maintenance, and fluctuating fuel prices.
  • Fuel economy figures are laboratory-tested values (WLTP or equivalent) sourced from EECA via Carjam. Real-world consumption typically differs — often significantly for EVs and hybrids.
  • CO₂ figures represent tailpipe emissions only and are sourced from manufacturer testing. They do not account for upstream emissions from fuel or electricity generation.
  • Safety ratings are sourced from Carjam, which references ANCAP and other test bodies. Ratings apply to the tested variant and year — individual vehicles may differ due to modifications, damage, or equipment differences.
  • Vehicle price estimates are based on historical Trade Me Motors averages for the make, model, and year. They are not valuations and may not reflect current market conditions, vehicle condition, or specific features.
  • WOF status and odometer readings are sourced from the NZTA register via Carjam and reflect the most recently reported data, which may not be current.
  • Fuel and electricity prices are manually maintained averages and may not reflect prices in your region or on the date of your visit.
  • Road User Charges (RUC) rates are based on current NZTA published rates and are subject to change.
